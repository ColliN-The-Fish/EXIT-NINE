中文介绍

1996 年，美国北部一处偏僻的高速服务区。亚历克斯为了凑齐新住处的首付款，接下三个夜班。卸货、补货、结账，本应只是重复的工作；但有些客人的资料没有问题，他们的身体和行为却不对劲。更糟的是，有东西开始学你说话，学你工作。

你要做什么

  • 签收配送、逐箱验货，把货物搬到对应货架；在工作与接客之间安排时间。
  • 等顾客挑货、排队，逐件扫描商品、收现金并找零。金额不对时可以撤回重找，找齐后自动结束交易。
  • 观察顾客的动作，核对证件、电脑资料、说法和访问权限。真实证件不等于对方可信，也不代表他可以进入员工区。
  • 使用监控、手电、地图和公告板上的员工手册，处理加油求助、停电与异常请求。
  • 在危机中关门、加固、躲藏或使用有限弹药自保，确认接应以后离开。

三晚剧情

下面只给出开端；展开后包含主线和结局信息。

亚历克斯原有 $120，目标是凑齐 $300 租房首付款。个人工资进入租房基金，店铺资金单独用于补给。故事从末班公交抵达、换制服和上一班店员本的交接开始。

展开完整三晚剧情与结局（含剧透）

第一晚：它在听你说话。 正常营业之余，辨认顾客，拒绝不合理的后仓和替班请求。收尾时，本打来电话，询问“你”为什么叫他明晚提前换班——亚历克斯根本没有打过这通电话。监控记录揭示，有客人在搬货间隙使用柜台座机，模仿了亚历克斯的声音。主角与老板取消假调班，正常领薪离开。风险看似已被排除，钱也确实到账，他决定再做一班。

第二晚：谁还在上班。 天气恶化。营业结束、局部停电后，黑暗里还传来拖箱和补货的声音。循声查看，亚历克斯发现一个穿员工服的人影。固定镜头中的质问让它露出破绽：它先学本说话，又开始重复主角自己的声音。被识破后，它变形并迅速逃出正门，玩家才可以恢复供电。主角明确辞职、停止营业；但暴雪和公路事故切断返程交通。他关店等待安全接应，白天在员工室轮休。第三晚不是为了钱主动回来冒险。

第三晚：你等的那个人来了。 商店保持停业，没有普通购物流程。县道路联络通过原来的频道说明接应：N9 车辆，从店前公路左侧来，停在原公交站。门外却先后传来“本”的声音和一个声称车在后面的接应者。通过监控与原频道回拨，主角确认是假接应。伪装被识破，模仿者用主角第一晚的话回应，袭击随之开始。玩家独自自保，等待真正的 N9 到站，核实车辆和位置后，从前场前往公交站上车。

结局。 钱够就搬进新住处；钱不够也能离开，回到旅馆，换个地方继续挣。安全撤离不以达到租房金额为条件。入夜，服务区的灯再次亮起，柜台后传来熟悉的声音：“第九出口。这里还有人值班。”主角离开了，但服务区的事情没有彻底结束。

English introduction

1996. A remote highway service station in the northern United States. Alex takes three night shifts to earn the first payment for a new place to live. Unload deliveries, stock shelves, and serve customers. The paperwork may be correct, but some visitors do not move or behave like people. Something is learning your voice—and learning your job.

How to play

  • Sign for deliveries, inspect boxes, and restock the matching shelves while keeping the shop running.
  • Let customers browse and queue. Scan each item, accept their cash, and count out change. Undo an unfinished change selection if you make a mistake; the transaction ends automatically when the amount is correct.
  • Compare physical behavior, IDs, computer records, statements, and access permissions. A valid identity does not automatically make someone trustworthy—or authorize access to the stockroom.
  • Use CCTV, your flashlight, the map, and the employee handbook on the noticeboard to investigate problems.
  • Deal with fuel-pump requests, power failures, and suspicious visitors. During a crisis, lock doors, reinforce entrances, hide, or use limited ammunition. Verify your pickup before leaving.

Story across the three nights

Alex starts with $120 and needs $300 for the first housing payment. Personal wages go into a housing fund; shop supplies use separate business funds. The opening follows the last bus, a change of uniform, and the handover from Ben, the previous clerk.

Read the complete three-night story and endings — spoilers

Night One — It is listening to you. Alex handles ordinary customers and suspicious requests. At closing, Ben calls about a request supposedly made by Alex to start the next shift early. Alex never made that call. CCTV reveals a visitor using the counter telephone while Alex was moving stock—and speaking in Alex's voice. The false shift change is cancelled and Alex receives the first night's pay. With the immediate trick exposed, returning for another paid shift seems manageable.

Night Two — Who is still working? The weather deteriorates. After business ends and part of the power fails, boxes continue scraping and products are still being placed on shelves in the dark. Alex follows the sounds and finds a figure in an employee uniform. During a fixed cinematic confrontation, it copies Ben's voice, then repeats Alex's own words in Alex's voice. Exposed, it changes shape and races out through the front entrance. Only then can Alex restore power. Alex quits and closes the shop, but a snowstorm and highway accidents have stopped return transport. He stays inside to wait for a safe pickup and rests during the day.

Night Three — The person you were waiting for. The shop remains closed; this night is about leaving, not serving more customers. The original road-dispatch channel specifies an N9 vehicle approaching from the left side of the road and stopping at the bus stop in front. First, a voice claiming to be Ben asks for entry. Then a purported rescuer says the vehicle is behind the building. CCTV and a call back on the original channel expose the deception. The impostor repeats something Alex said on the first night, and the attack begins. Alex must protect himself until the real N9 arrives, verify the vehicle and location, and reach the front bus stop alone.

The ending. With enough money, Alex moves into a new home. Otherwise, he returns to the motel and decides to earn the rest elsewhere. Reaching the housing target is not required to escape. After dark, the station lights come on again. A familiar voice behind the counter says: “Exit Nine. Someone is still on duty.” Alex has left, but the station's story is not over.

Controls and availability

WASD + mouse to move and look; Shift to run; E to interact; Q to put down a box, return an ID, or end a conversation. F toggles the flashlight; B uses binoculars, with mouse-wheel zoom. G draws the handgun; left-click fires while it is drawn. M opens the map; Tab opens notes; Backspace undoes unfinished change; Esc pauses or exits a device view.

Desktop Chrome with a keyboard, mouse, and headphones is recommended. The current in-game interface, subtitles, and synthetic voices are in Chinese. This project introduction is bilingual. Progress is saved locally for the same browser and origin. Saves from localhost do not automatically transfer to the online site.
